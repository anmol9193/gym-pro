MORE CLARITY.

Your questions about HIIT & Cardio.

Is this only for experienced members?+

Talk about your current experience and preferences with the team before choosing a starting point. The aim is to find a suitable direction for your training.

Can I combine cardio with strength training?+

You can discuss a mix of activities with the gym team. The program and membership pages show the available directions and existing class inclusions.

Which plan includes group classes?+

Basic includes two group classes per month. Pro and Elite include unlimited group classes. Confirm the activities available through your chosen plan with the gym.
