Can I join if I am a beginner?+

Yes. Tell the team about your current experience and goals so they can help you find an appropriate starting point and training pace.

Can I explore more than one program?+

You can discuss a mix of activities with the team. Basic includes two group classes per month, while Pro includes unlimited group classes. Confirm the activities covered by your chosen membership before joining.

Is personal training included in my membership?+

The Elite membership includes four personal training sessions per month. Visit the membership section to compare the existing Basic, Pro and Elite plans.

How do I choose the right program?+

Explore each program's focus and audience, then use its enquiry button to prepare your interest in the main form. If you are unsure, choose "Help me find my starting point" in the enquiry form.
