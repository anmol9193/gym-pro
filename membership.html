A little clarity for your next step.

Which membership should I start with?+

Basic covers gym floor access and two group classes each month. Pro adds unlimited classes, a diet plan and steam room access. Elite includes everything in Pro plus personal training, body analysis and guest passes.

Can I try the gym before choosing a plan?+

The website offers a first-week trial. Prepare an enquiry to discuss your visit and confirm trial inclusions and session availability with the gym.

How many personal training sessions does Elite include?+

Elite includes four personal training sessions per month, alongside the features included in Pro.

Does choosing a plan take a payment?+

No. Choosing a plan opens the Contact page with that membership selected. You can prepare an enquiry draft to discuss the plan; this website does not process payments.
