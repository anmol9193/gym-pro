YOUR QUESTIONS.
OUR APPROACH.

Start with the information you need.

Do I need experience before joining?+

No. Share your current experience and goals with the team so you can discuss a suitable starting point and training pace.

How do I choose where to start?+

Read the program descriptions or select "Help me find my starting point" in the enquiry form. Your interests and everyday routine are useful starting points for the conversation.

Does everyone follow the same training journey?+

No. The example roadmap shows how habits can develop over time. Your training pace, goals and progress will depend on your own circumstances.

What if my goals change?+

Bring it up with your coach or the gym team. Reviewing your goals and discussing a different direction is part of building a routine that works for you.
