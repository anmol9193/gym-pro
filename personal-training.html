You can start the conversation at any experience level. Tell the team what you want help with and discuss an appropriate starting point.

Is personal training part of Elite?+

Elite includes four personal training sessions per month, along with the existing Pro features. The membership page compares the plans.

Yes. Add your goal to the optional message in your enquiry draft so you have a clear starting point for a conversation with the gym.
